## G2-C1 — evidence, the deprecation contract and history hygiene (2026-10-09)

Starting reported HEAD `339c34d`; after the rewrite, `fd2f288`. Three responsibilities, nothing else.

**1. `g2-a-view-does-not-keep-its-buffer`.** Root cause of Q1's NO VERDICT: a *weak killer*, blind on
Linux — not an equivalent mutant, not a reclamation defect. Without the view's retain the buffer
is freed while the view lives (a use after free). macOS's allocator traps on the view's later
release (exit 133); glibc's per-thread cache keeps the freed chunk's data and skips
`MALLOC_PERTURB_`, so the view still read its 7 and the counts balanced. The killer
(`every_buffer_is_reclaimed_by_its_last_view_everywhere`) now makes four same-size buffers after
the loop, each marked where the view reads, so a freed buffer handed out again shows through.
Disposition: kept, killer repaired, no replacement. **CAUGHT**, tier 1, campaign
`fd920f457faa89b4`, killer verified in-session on Linux (pristine pass, mutant fail, restored pass).

**2. `@deprecated`: metadata-only deprecation.** On a `pub` function, `struct` or `enum` only
(`N0619` elsewhere — before a private function, a constant, a trait or an impl, where nothing would
read it); `nazm doc` publishes it as the item's `deprecated` in `nazm.api-doc/2` (additive; the
reason, or empty) and as a line in the Markdown. No diagnostic reports a use; warning severity was
deliberately **not** added (one severity in 1.0). Tests: `attributes.rs`
`deprecation_is_metadata_the_api_documentation_publishes` (JSON and Markdown, a use compiles with
empty stderr) and four refusals; `schemas.rs` validates real output. Mutants
`g2c1-a-deprecation-is-not-published`, `g2c1-deprecated-may-mark-anything`, and `n38-the-epoch-stays`
repointed: campaign `31c639022ecbdb60`, 3 of 3 caught at tier 1, killers verified. The compatibility
case `run/attributes-cfg.nz`, added in Gate 2T and never in an accepted baseline, now marks a `pub`
function and is re-pinned.
