copyright and licensed it to the project; on 2026-11-07 the copyright holder decided that a
contributor **assigns** the copyright instead (see `decisions-log.md`). This text was written
without legal review; it is to be reviewed by a lawyer before the first outside contribution is
accepted, and no contribution is accepted under it before then. Points the lawyer must settle are

the Nazm project, now or later, until you end it under section 11.

## 1. Definitions

- **Contribution** — any work of authorship, including any change to an existing work, that you

  examples, designs and their accompanying data, through a pull request, patch, issue, comment or
  any other channel the project uses. A work you mark in writing as *"Not a Contribution"* when you
  submit it is not one.
- **Nazm** — the Nazm language, its specification, toolchain, standard library, runtime and the

## How to sign

**Individuals** sign through a bot. On your first pull request, a check named `cla` asks you to
sign; reply to the pull request with exactly this sentence, from the account that made it:

```text
I have read the Nazm Contributor Licence Agreement, version 0.2, and I agree to it.
```

The bot records your GitHub account, the pull request and the time, and the check then passes on
this and every later pull request. The record is kept in the Nazm repository
(`cla/signatures.json` on the `cla-signatures` branch; `.github/workflows/cla.yml`). The Maintainer
also keeps the version of this text each signature refers to. What the record holds — your GitHub
account name and number and the time — is kept for as long as the agreement is in force, to prove
the assignment, and is not used for anything else.

**An entity** signing for its employees sends the Maintainer a signed document naming the entity,
the person signing for it and proof that they may bind it, and the people whose Contributions it
covers, and tells the Maintainer when that list changes; each of those people then also signs through
the bot. The Maintainer keeps a record of every signature.

A new version of this agreement is announced in the repository, and a contributor signs it before
their next Contribution under it.
